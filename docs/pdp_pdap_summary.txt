PDP and PDAP Summary

Formula:
PDP  = Total Power × Clock Period
PDAP = Total Power × Clock Period × Area

Note:
Power is in mW.
Clock period is in ns.
Area is in um².
PDP unit = pJ.
PDAP unit = pJ·um².
